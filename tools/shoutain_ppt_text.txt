Story Premise & Core Mechanic
The story starts when Rebecca Shen enters Longevity Pagoda Village. When she scans the “Shou Contract,” she is registered—“named” by the contract. From that moment, the village begins to “see” her through an ancient, feudal logic: rules, taboos, and
ritual obligations.
The whole village carries a deliberate sense of staged authenticity—strict etiquette, heavy rituals, performative 
      tradition. But you also notice some modern items. This contrast hints that the “heritage ritual” is actually a curated     
      performance.
As the player progresses, the mask peels off: the Longevity Pagoda is 
     not a miracle, but a money-making vessel built by the local. The 
     villagers' greed made them willing to cover up the deaths at the 
     construction site, and even send the poor little girl to the altar, in order
     to gain "longevity" and "wealth".
Greed turns people cruel
             — and rituals can become tools for exploitation.
Core mechanism -- WATCH Value (a variable that changes according to the player's choice): A quantification of "social pressure”:  

     Players have to make a trade-off between "revealing the truth" and
     "safe advancement".

Project 1c Upgrade: Sensory Immersion
Voice Integration
Character dialogue is now fully voice-enhanced using ElevenLabs technology. This implementation brings each character to life with unique, high-quality voices that match their distinct personalities and roles within the game world.
First-Person Village Entrance Video
A short first-person entrance video is added at the beginning of the game.Instead of only seeing the village as a background image, the player visually “walks into” Longevity Pagoda Village.
Dynamic In-Game Triggering
Voice lines are not just static overlays. They are dynamically triggered during gameplay to align perfectly with key story beats and critical player choices.
KEY IMPACT: Turning text choices into immersive, humanized interactions that deepen player investment in the story and characters.

Project 1c Experiment: 360° Panoramic Village
● Experiment: Tested Skybox.AI to generate 360° panoramic scenes of Longevity Pagoda Village.
● Result: The generated visual style did not fully match the intended gloomy and uncanny atmosphere.
● Design Insight: Immersion requires not only advanced technology, but also stylistic consistency with the narrative world.

=== Slide 14 ===

Research paper: drama llama mechanics
Triggers are IF-THEN rules based on game state.
Drama Llama uses triggers plus reusable storylets to assemble narrative dynamically. 
Player actions hit conditions, and the system selects the next suitable storylet.
